**Last updated:** November 16, 2026

xstate is operated by **HireNinja LLC**. Paid plans (Growth) are sold by our reseller **Paddle.com**,
the merchant of record for all our orders. Paddle handles payments, invoices, sales tax and refunds.

## Cancel any time

You can cancel a paid plan at any time from **Settings → Billing**. Your plan stays active until the end of the
period you've paid for, then your organization moves to the Free plan. Your mail, files and other data stay where
they are; nothing is deleted.

## 14-day refunds

- **New subscriptions.** You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment.
- **Renewals.** You can ask for a full refund of a monthly or yearly renewal within 14 days of the renewal
  payment.
- **Seats and upgrades.** Charges for added people or for switching to a higher plan can be refunded within 14
  days of the charge.

After 14 days, payments are not refundable, except where the law requires otherwise.

## How to ask for a refund

- Reply to the receipt email from Paddle, or use the link in it to contact Paddle directly, or
- email [hello@xstate.com](mailto:hello@xstate.com) from your organization's owner or admin account and tell us
  which payment you'd like refunded.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. When
a subscription payment is refunded, the paid plan ends and your organization moves to the Free plan.

## Your statutory rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under consumer law where you live. Paddle's
[buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms) also apply to purchases made through Paddle.
