title: Quickstart
description: From sign-up to a working inbox on your domain.
---

## 1. Create an account

Sign up at [go.xstate.com/signup](https://go.xstate.com/signup) with your email and a password, or with Google.

## 2. Add your domain

Enter a domain you own, for example `yourcompany.com`.

## 3. Create addresses

Pick the addresses you want, such as `hello@` and your own name.

## 4. Verify the domain

xstate shows the DNS records your domain needs. If your DNS is at a provider that supports Domain Connect, such as
Cloudflare, one click writes them for you. Otherwise copy them into your DNS provider; xstate checks them
automatically.

## 5. Connect an agent

Connect Claude, Claude Code, Codex or Cursor over MCP and choose what it may do.
