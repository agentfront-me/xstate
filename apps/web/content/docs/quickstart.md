## 1. Create an account

Sign up at [go.xstate.com/signup](https://go.xstate.com/signup) with your email and a password.

## 2. Add your domain

Enter a domain you own, for example `yourcompany.com`.

## 3. Create addresses

Pick the addresses you want, such as `hello@` and your own name.

## 4. Verify the domain

xstate shows the DNS records your domain needs. Copy them into your DNS provider, or copy them all at once as a
zone file, which most providers can import. xstate checks public DNS every few seconds and shows each record as it
appears. Most domains are ready within a few minutes.

One-click setup through Domain Connect is rolling out, starting with Cloudflare. When it is available for your
domain, xstate shows a button that adds the records for you.

## 5. Connect an AI app

Connect Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or ChatGPT over MCP and choose what it may do. See
[Connect AI apps](./mcp.md).

## 6. Send email from your server

To send receipts, alerts or sign-in codes from your own code, create a personal token and call the API. See
[Send transactional email](./transactional-email.md).
