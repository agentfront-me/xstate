---
title: Connected AI apps, email API, pricing
summary: Connect Claude, Codex, Cursor or ChatGPT over MCP, send transactional email from your own mailboxes, and new plans.
date: 2026-11-16
---

- **Connected AI apps.** Claude, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and ChatGPT can connect to your mail, calendar, files and
  contacts over MCP. You choose the mailboxes and what each app may do, and sending is off by default.
  [Connect AI apps](/docs/mcp).
- **Email API.** `POST /v1/emails` sends receipts, alerts and other one-to-one email from any mailbox you can send
  from, with personal tokens limited to the mailboxes you pick. [Send transactional email](/docs/transactional-email).
- **Plans.** Free, Pro at $6 and Team at $12 per person a month. Shared mailboxes are free.
- **Sign-in.** xstate.com is now the website; the app lives at go.xstate.com. Old links redirect.
