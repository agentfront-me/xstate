---
title: Pricing by the people you reach
summary: No more charges per seat. Free covers 1,000 people a month; Growth is $49 for 5,000 and grows with you.
date: 2026-11-19
---

- **New plans.** Plans are now priced by the people your organization reaches each month, not by seats. Everyone
  you email or who emails you counts once a month, plus website visitors who have signed up or filled in a form.
  Anonymous visitors and mail in Junk never count. [See pricing](/#pricing).
- **Free:** 1,000 people a month, 5 GB of storage, 5 mailboxes, 3 domains, 4,000 emails a month and one agent on
  your own AI key.
- **Growth:** $49 a month for 5,000 people with 50 GB, then $1 and 1 GB for every 100 more people. Unlimited
  mailboxes, domains, emails and agents.
- **Custom:** for organizations reaching more than a million people a month. [Talk to us](mailto:hello@xstate.com).
- **Usage in Settings → Billing.** See people reached (by email and from your website), storage, emails, mailboxes,
  domains, agents and website events this month against your plan. Owners and admins get an email as a limit
  gets close.
- **Payments by Stripe.** Upgrade, change your size and manage invoices and your card from Settings → Billing.
  Cancel any time, with a full refund within 14 days of any payment.
